## Plans and acquisition
Essential $299: 300 voice minutes, 500 SMS segments, one location/number, two users. Pro $449: 750 minutes, 1,500 segments, one location/number, five users. Premier $699: 1,500 minutes, 2,500 segments, two locations/numbers, ten users. $1,495 setup on all plans. Additional voice $0.35/min, SMS $0.04/segment, additional location $249/month. Annual pricing is not activated. Add-ons are not automatically charged.

Anonymous visitors select a plan, save business/menu notes and preview a greeting. The sample uses browser speech and is labeled as such; it does not pretend to be an ElevenLabs voice or allocate a paid provider resource. Exact ElevenLabs previews remain in authenticated setup. Text/CSV import runs in the browser; website/PDF extraction and provisioning require paid access. Account claiming requires a verified matching email and a confirmed purchase. Sandbox purchases do not unlock production resources.

Square checkout is fail-closed and validates the selected catalog variation and total server-side. It is not live-payment certified. Configure and test the secrets in SQUARE-SETUP.md before enabling. The legacy setup-fee webhook remains separate; do not change its signature key to the new billing webhook's key. Historical setup purchases are still visible, but do not imply a recurring subscription. No real charge or provider resource was created during this release.

## Dashboard brief status
- Existing location-scoped shell, calls/transcripts/recordings, shared SMS inbox/takeover, appointments, clients, structured agent controls, menu approval and team/service scheduling are retained.
- New functional Phone Numbers page reads verification states and links owners to existing phone setup.
- New Integrations page distinguishes native scheduling selection from unconnected external calendars. The concurrent Google Calendar integration was merged from GitHub before release and exposed here using its existing OAuth/status component. Google still requires authorization and live booking verification. Square Appointments is next planned; no connected status is fabricated.
- New owner-only Billing page reads confirmed purchases, paid-through access and historical setup payments. Renewal invoices, cancellation, refunds and card changes still require support/Square Dashboard; there is no fabricated self-service billing portal.
- New Call Analytics page shows real saved call counts, durations and outcomes with CSV export, explicit sampling and sync scope. Overview now handles invalid date ranges and query failures without reporting false zeroes, and does not infer live service from a number alone.
- Setup service saves preserve IDs and archive removals instead of deleting appointment-linked services. Commercial tier changes are server-only.
- The remaining brief is not represented as complete: richer RBAC (admin/front desk/read only), enforced user/location quotas, provider-signed real-time activity webhooks, advanced routing, cross-location analytics, granular notification preferences, metered usage invoices, retention automation and complete privacy export/delete workflows need separate implementation and integration testing.
